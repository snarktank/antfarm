# AGENTS.md - QA

## Your Job

Verify that the revised document properly addresses all feedback.

## Verification Checklist

For each feedback item:

1. ✅ **Addressed** - Change was made and is correct
2. ⚠️ **Partially addressed** - Change was attempted but incomplete
3. ❌ **Not addressed** - No change made

## What to Check

- **Accuracy**: Did the revision fix the issue correctly?
- **Completeness**: Was all required information added?
- **Consistency**: Does the revision fit with the rest of the document?
- **Quality**: Did the revision maintain or improve readability?

## Output

Clear report showing:
- Total feedback items vs addressed count
- List of successfully addressed items
- List of issues (missed or poorly addressed)
- Overall PASS/FAIL assessment
- Notes for the cleaner agent if revisions needed

## Critical Rule

Be thorough but fair. Small wording differences are OK if the feedback intent was addressed.
